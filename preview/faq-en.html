Frequently asked questions

How do I create a oneD account?
  1. Open the oneD app or website, tap Log In, then tap Create Account
  2. Sign up with your email (set a password of at least 8 characters) or your phone number (enter the OTP code)
  3. Tap Confirm. You can start watching right away
How do I log in?

Tap Log In and use the same method you signed up with: email, phone number, Facebook, Google, Apple or an AIS number.

If you log in with a different method from the one you signed up with, it counts as a different account, and you won't see the package you subscribed to.

How do I edit my personal information?

Log in, then tap Profile (top right) › Edit

How do I log out?

Tap Profile (top right) › Log Out. On the website, tap OK to confirm.

How do I check if I have oneD PREMIUM?

Tap Profile

  • You see a Subscription menu = you have PREMIUM (tap it to see your package)
  • You see a Subscribe button = you don't have PREMIUM yet
How do I subscribe to oneD PREMIUM?
  1. Log in, then tap oneD PREMIUM in the top menu
  2. Choose a package and tap Subscribe Now
  3. Confirm the payment through the App Store or Google Play. PREMIUM starts right away
What do I get with oneD PREMIUM?
  • No ad breaks, and every episode
  • Full HD 1080p, on up to 2 devices at the same time
  • Cast to your TV with Chromecast and AirPlay
  • oneD Shorts vertical dramas (phones and tablets)
  • Picture in Picture and playback speed control

Terms and conditions as set by the company apply.

How do I watch oneD on TV?

You need oneD PREMIUM.

  1. Search for "oneD" in your TV's app store and install it:
    • Android TV / Google TV (Android 9+): Play Store
    • Samsung (2019 or newer, Tizen 5.0+): Home › APPS
    • LG (webOS 5.0+): LG Content Store
    • Apple TV (tvOS 17.6+): App Store
  2. Open the app and scan the QR code on the TV screen with your phone
  3. Log in with your PREMIUM account and start watching
How do I cast to my TV with Chromecast or AirPlay?
  1. Connect your phone and TV to the same Wi-Fi, and update the oneD app to the latest version
  2. Log in with your PREMIUM account and open what you want to watch
  3. Tap or and choose your TV
How do I redeem a PREMIUM top-up code?

Codes can only be redeemed in a web browser (not in the app).

  1. Go to redeem.oned.net and log in
    or go to www.oned.net › Profile › Redeem Code
  2. Enter the code (in capital letters), accept the Terms and Conditions, and tap Redeem Code
  3. Once it's redeemed, PREMIUM works on all your devices, including the app
Can I add more codes, or mix codes of different lengths?

Yes. You can add a code right away, even if you still have days left, and mix 1-, 7- and 30-day codes. The extra days are added to your expiry date.

You can redeem up to 10 codes per week.

Where can I see when my package expires?

Profile › Subscription › see Renewal Date

My code won't redeem
  • The account must not have a monthly/yearly package
  • Enter the code in capital letters only
  • The code hasn't expired, and you haven't redeemed more than 10 codes this week

Still not working? Contact our team and tell us where you got the code.

I have a monthly/yearly package. Can I redeem a top-up code?

Not yet. Create a new account for top-up codes, or wait until your current package expires.

I'm on top-up days. Can I subscribe monthly/yearly?

Yes, but we recommend waiting until your top-up days run out.

If you subscribe while you still have days left, you're charged right away and the remaining days are lost, with no compensation.

Video is buffering, freezing or won't play
  1. Update the oneD app to the latest version
  2. Check your internet or Wi-Fi connection
  3. Close and reopen the app (on the website, refresh the page)

Still not working? Contact our team

I can't log in
  1. Use the same log-in method you signed up with, and check your email and password
  2. If you use Facebook, Google or Apple, check that the account isn't suspended
  3. Update the app to the latest version and reopen it

Still can't log in? Contact our team

I paid, but I don't have PREMIUM yet
  1. Check you're logged in with the account you subscribed with
  2. Check that the payment went through
  3. Still no PREMIUM? Contact our team with a screenshot of the receipt email from Apple or Google Play
Cancel a package bought on iPhone / iPad

Cancel a package bought on Android

Google Play › your profile picture › Payments & Subscriptions › Subscriptions › oneD › Cancel Subscription

Cancel a package bought through AIS

myAIS app: Package › Add-on Packages or Additional Services › oneD › Cancel Package

Or call AIS Call Center 1175 and ask to cancel the oneD service billed to AIS.

Can I cancel top-up days?

There's nothing to cancel: top-up days are never charged automatically. When they run out, you go back to watching with ads. (You can't cancel before they expire.)

Can I use oneD outside Thailand?

oneD works in Thailand and in some other countries, depending on licensing. What you can watch may differ by country. Live TV is only available in Thailand.
